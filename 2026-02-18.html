The Damascus Moment

A man nearly blind—discouraged, hurting, and unsure of his worth—walked into the gathering of Gulfview Church. He could barely see the faces in front of him, but he came anyway, desperate for hope. He fell to his knees in desperation and cried out while people surrounded him in prayer.

A few days later, this man came to our outreach where JL brought a powerful word that he later shared spoke right to him. Volunteers prayed over him with the simple belief that God still heals. Days later, he returned to men's breakfast with a testimony that stunned everyone: his eyesight had begun to return. He described it as a “Damascus moment,” a sudden breaking-in of God’s mercy in the middle of his darkness.

"Healing comes in different ways. Some receive it instantly. Others walk it out slowly. Waiting does not mean God has forgotten—it means He is still moving." — Rachelle, Salty Talk Host

The Call to Care

For years, the ministry recognized a desperate need for medical care on the streets. God answered through a retired nurse who had been experiencing divine "nudges" in her dreams. By saying a simple "yes," she transformed the outreach, providing wound care and dignity to the unhoused community.
